How we think about security

Three principles behind every deployment.

Technology and equipment help, but the quality of a security operation is decided by people and process.

01

People before technology

Cameras and alarms only work when trained people respond well. We invest first in recruitment, training and supervision.

02

Process before presence

A guard standing at a gate is not security by itself. Clear post orders, records and escalation make presence effective.

03

Supervision before promises

We would rather show you how a post is supervised than promise perfection. Regular checks are how standards are kept.

Our standards

How we keep quality high.

Good security is a habit. These six practices shape how we work.

Disciplined recruitment

We recruit with care, looking for people who are disciplined, honest and ready to learn.

Rigorous training

Every guard is trained before deployment and receives guidance as your site needs change.

Verification

Personnel are verified before they are placed at your site.

Regular supervision

Supervisors and managers visit posts and act quickly on feedback.

Courtesy

We expect respect and politeness towards every visitor, resident and customer.

Customization

We design each deployment around your premises rather than using a fixed template.
