Overview

Armed Security that you can rely on.

Disciplined armed personnel for locations that need a higher level of protection, deployed with clear procedures and strict supervision.

Armed deployment is a serious responsibility, not simply an upgrade. It calls for the right legal framework, carefully selected personnel, strict rules on when and how a weapon may be handled, and constant supervision. Our approach is to start with the risk, decide whether armed cover is truly needed, and then build a deployment that is lawful, disciplined and proportionate.

Risks we plan for

Where things go wrong, and how we prevent it.

Every site has its own weak points. These are the ones we look for first.

01

Licence and legal compliance

Armed cover must follow the rules that apply to your site and location. Gaps here create liability for everyone involved.

02

Improper handling

Poor discipline around weapons is a risk in itself. Selection, training and supervision must be strict.

03

Over reliance on arms

A weapon is not a substitute for good access control, patrols and awareness. It works best as one layer of a wider plan.

04

Unclear rules of conduct

Personnel need clear instructions on escalation, restraint and reporting so that decisions are consistent under pressure.

What good looks like

The standard you should expect.

These are the signs of a well run deployment. Hold us to them.

  • The need for armed cover is justified by a documented risk assessment.
  • Personnel are verified, trained and supervised to a defined standard.
  • Rules of conduct and reporting are written down and briefed.
  • Armed and unarmed staff work to one coordinated plan.

FAQ

Questions about armed security.

Cannot find your answer? Call +91 91059 09006 or email info@parakramindia.org.

Are armed guards licensed?

Armed deployments follow the rules and licence requirements that apply to your site and location. We explain what is needed during the site assessment.

When is armed security needed?

It is usually chosen for high value assets, sensitive facilities or locations where a stronger deterrent is needed. We help you decide during the assessment.

Can armed and unarmed guards work together?

Yes. Many sites combine both under one plan, with clear roles for each.

How do you decide whether armed cover is necessary?

We begin with a risk assessment of your assets, location and threat profile. If armed cover is not needed, we will say so and recommend a more suitable plan.

How is discipline maintained around weapons?

Through careful selection, clear written rules, regular briefings and close supervision. Discipline is treated as a daily habit, not a one time event.
