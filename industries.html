01 / Industrial

Security for industrial sites.

Plants, warehouses and industrial campuses lose most to leakage of material, uncontrolled vehicle movement and gaps at shift changes. We plan gate control, patrols and records around how your operation actually runs, and we make sure guards understand your safety rules as well as your security rules.

  • Gate and perimeter control
  • Vehicle and material checks
  • Shift wise deployment
  • Patrols across large areas
